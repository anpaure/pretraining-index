Technical Report · Anpaure Pretraining Index (ANPI) v1

Measuring Pretraining Perplexity Through a Black Box

A zero-logprob, strictly zero-CoT continuation-ranking benchmark across 954 post-cutoff Nov-2026 items—placing 105 live open and closed endpoints from 18 labs on one scale with 0.85% within-OpenAI LOO and 3.41% cross-lab LOLO across 7 independent open labs.

On November 22, 2026, OpenAI’s chief scientist Jakub Pachocki argued in Perplexity and Intelligence that base-model log-perplexity is the cleanest single-number measure of pretraining progress—but released no public dataset. Two days later, Mercor proposed averaging six public multiple-choice exams as a proxy, showing a −0.98 Spearman correlation on nine OpenAI models and immediately raising the key question: does a downstream exam average actually measure pretraining compression across labs, or mostly post-training and time trends?

We built the Anpaure Pretraining Index (ANPI): a direct black-box 8-choice continuation-ranking evaluation over N = 954 provably post-cutoff November 2026 items (908 unique source URLs, 5 domains) against neutral 24B cloud decoys, audited at strict 0 / 99,921 reasoning tokens across 105 live endpoints from 18 labs (68 closed frontier endpoints + 37 open-weight endpoints, alongside 11 Elie-only open checkpoints for 116 ranked models total). Scoring models by zero-fit Pairwise Rank AUC and calibrating Thurstone Case V discriminability d′ = √2 Φ−1(AUC) onto ln(BPB) outperforms Mercor, SimpleQA-Verified, and AA-Omniscience-Accuracy on predictive error across both OpenAI and cross-lab open ground truth:

1. Why Direct Logprobs and MCQ Shortcuts Both Break

On open weights, exact teacher-forced bits-per-byte (BPB) is straightforward—Elie Bakouch’s Pre-training Index measures it cleanly across 27 open checkpoints. On closed frontier APIs, direct logprob evaluation fails twice over: first, Anthropic and Google omit logprobs completely and OpenAI disables echo=true prompt-token logprobs; second, post-training RL sharpening collapses raw next-token logprob BPB. In our early logprob experiments (pplx_protocol_a/), raw API BPB correlated negatively (r = −0.136) with true pretraining quality across RL-tuned endpoints because overconfident wrong tokens blow up −log p.

Downstream exam averages avoid logprobs but run into three opposite problems: saturation (Claude Fable 5.2 already hits 90.4% on Mercor and 94.0% on SimpleQA-Verified), reasoning leakage (Mercor’s o3 scores 69.1% vs. identical-base GPT-4.1 at 48.1%), and untested cross-lab validity.

2. Method: Rank-Coded Continuation Ranking (ANPI v1)

  1. Provably post-cutoff 5-domain corpus (N = 954, 908 unique URLs): Every source document carries a November 2026 timestamp across News/Speeches (199 Hansard & BBC), Research Papers (198 arXiv & bioRxiv), Essays (198 Guardian & LessWrong), newly created Wikipedia articles (197), and newly added single-line code blocks (162 across 116 distinct Nov-2026 commits in CPython, Linux, Postgres, Go, LLVM, PyTorch, ClickHouse, FFmpeg, etc.).
  2. Neutral 24B cloud decoys (K = 8): At each cut point, we keep the authentic next snippet (~6 prose words or ~36 bytes of code) and sample 7 distinct T = 1.0 raw continuations from mistral-small-24b-instruct-2501 via /api/v1/completions (fixing the trailing-space BPE boundary bug), with zero model-family overlap with any calibration anchor.
  3. Strict 8-letter permutation & RL sharpening nuance: Each API receives the shuffled candidates A..H and must return an exact 8-distinct-letter permutation (C,A,F,B,H,D,E,G). Global monotone sharpening preserves the ordering of candidate likelihoods p(y|x)—though note it does not preserve likelihood-ratio ordering p(y|x)/q(y|x) when q is nonuniform, and rankings alone require empirical anchor calibration to map onto absolute BPB.
  4. Strict Zero-CoT across all 105 live endpoints (0 / 99,921 items): Reasoning tokens are suppressed across every provider and verified with A/B zero-bias controls: explicit no-scratchpad prompting (100-item Gemini 4 Argon control, p = 0.85); forced tool_choice on o3, GLM-5.3 Flash, and Inkling; 6-shot + effort: minimal on Grok 4.7 (100-item Grok 4.20 A/B z = −0.85); named_tool + empty thinking on Grok 4.5 / 4.6 (z = −1.06); {"order":" assistant prefill on Gemini 2.5 Pro & Step 5 Preview (Gemini 2.5 Flash A/B z = −1.18, 0/4 hard-math litmus); constant empty-ATEM header on Meta Muse Wonder (Vertex AI, byte-identical to "Reply OK"); and raw /completions zero-think templates on 6 mandatory-reasoning open endpoints (badged BYPASS).
  5. Automated 15-metric trace-health verification: Every live model is checked by tests/test_trace_health.py and Section 7 of audit_v1.py for slot primacy/recency bias, permutation diversity, prompt-example echo, single-domain collapse, and tail-rank inversion—with all top-25 frontier models 100% pathology-free.

3. Why Pairwise AUC + Thurstone d′ → ln(BPB)

Testing 24 zero-extra-parameter linking functions and three 3-parameter scaling laws across four independent datasets (Mistral-24B 5-domain + our earlier OLMo-1B Hansard suite, crossed against both OpenAI and Elie ground truth) revealed three clean results:

A. Real 8-way rank histograms follow Thurstone Case V (6.8× better than Plackett-Luce)

Because each candidate snippet spans ~8–12 tokens, its log-probability Sm(y) = Σ log pm(yt | x, y<t) is a sum of token log-probs—Gaussian by the Central Limit Theorem, not Gumbel. Matching a single number (AUC) per model and predicting its full 8-bin rank histogram P(r = 1..8) across real rank rows gives mean KL = 0.0057 nats under Thurstone Case V vs. 0.0391 nats under Plackett-Luce (6.8× better fit). Under Thurstone Case V, any global temperature scaling βm cancels identically in Signal Detection Theory’s discriminability:

AUCm = (8 − r̄m) / 7 = P(Sm(ytrue) > Sm(ydecoy)),     d′m = √2 · Φ−1(AUCm)

B. Tail ranks (r ≥ 2) carry massive independent BPB signal

Even after controlling for Top-1 accuracy, a model’s conditional mean rank among misses (r ≥ 2) retains a partial correlation of +0.730 on 24B-OpenAI, +0.761 on 1B-Elie, and +0.734 on 1B-OpenAI with true BPB. MRR puts ~75% of its variance on r = 1 vs. r ≥ 2—and binary MCQ throws away ranks 2–8 entirely. Switching from −ln(MRR) to pairwise AUC jumped our 10-model OpenAI Spearman correlation from +0.891 → +0.988 and cut LOO error from 1.18% → 0.85%.

C. Eliminating compute in Ruan et al. (NeurIPS 2024) gives exact log-BPB linearity

In Ruan, Maddison & Hashimoto (NeurIPS 2024), ln(BPB) ∝ ln C and σ−1(Acc) ∝ ln C. Eliminating compute yields the exact 2-parameter log-BPB law:

ln(BPBm) = a + b · (−d′m) = a − b · √2 · Φ−1(AUCm)

Across all 4 independent datasets (including OLMo-1B where frontier models hit AUC = 0.94), probit_auc [logY] ranked #1 of 24 transforms (3.11% 4-dataset average vs. 4.02% for −ln(MRR)), showed zero statistically significant curvature on all 4 datasets (mean |t2| = 1.09), and beat every 3-parameter literature curve (Gadre et al. ICLR 2025 Eq. 5, Ruan floor-sigmoid, Chinchilla E0), all of which overfitted on n = 10–14 anchors.

4. Validation & Variance Decomposition

On all 10 published OpenAI Nov-22 base models (GPT-4o → GPT-6.2 Astra), ANPI achieves r = +0.9895, ρ = +0.9879, and 0.85% blind LOO error (max 1.79%; blind upward extrapolation from GPT-4o..5.6 Sol to GPT-6 / 6.2 Astra: 1.44%). Controlling for Epoch’s ECI across OpenAI models retains partial r(ANPI, log OAI | ECI) = +0.967; on Elie’s open models, controlling for the AA Intelligence Index retains partial r(ANPI, Elie | AA Index) = +0.758 vs. −0.049 for AA Index.

On Elie’s 14 native-chat open models across 7 labs, we obtain r = +0.9113, ρ = +0.8418, LOO = 2.87%, and 7-lab Leave-One-Lab-Out LOLO = 3.41% (including the 3 visible Elie BYPASS models across 9 labs: LOO = 3.27%, LOLO = 3.65%). Decomposing total residual variance reveals why within-lab error is ~0.9% while cross-lab error floors around ~3.2%:

Within-OpenAI (10 models)0.94%0.79% (71.1% var)0.50% (28.9% var)

Inside OpenAI, 71% of our tiny 0.85% error is simply finite-sample noise from N = 954 items. Across labs, 84% is a genuine family-level offset (between-family bias SD = 3.16% vs. within-family scatter 1.96%)—each lab’s domain mix and post-training style shifts its whole family by ~1–5%.

5. Two Methodological Traps Exposed by Our Experiments

Trap 1 — "Minimal reasoning" leaks CoT and flips frontier rankings: Stripping hidden reasoning tokens to strict 0 dropped Gemini 4 Neon (old 953/953 items had r_tok > 0) by −0.0184 AUC / −0.0261 MRR (p = 0.0004), o3 by −0.0203 AUC (p = 0.029), and GLM-5.3 Flash by −0.0181 AUC (p = 0.046). With leaked CoT, Neon sat above GPT-6.2 Astra (matching Mercor); at strict 0-CoT, GPT-6.2 Astra cleanly beats Gemini 4 Neon (p = 0.024).
Trap 2 — Optimizing benchmark combinations on OpenAI (SOCP / |I| ≤ 3) collapses cross-lab: Running an exact nonnegative SOCP and global |I| ≤ 3 subset search on Epoch AI’s Nov-25 database (90 CSVs; 8 complete 11/11 OpenAI benchmarks) picks ARC-AGI-2 alone (r = +0.981 on OpenAI) or 0.74·ARC-AGI-2 + 0.18·SimpleQA + 0.08·Chess (r = +0.989). Cross-lab on Elie’s exact BPB, ARC-AGI-2 collapses to r = +0.088 (−0.210 at best effort), Chess and AIME go negative (−0.126, −0.251), and GPQA drops to +0.221. Worse, searching |I| ≤ 3 over 25–40 pure Gaussian noise columns on n = 11 targets produces fake r = +0.81–+0.88 (SOCP: +0.991).

7. Honest Limitations & Roadmap to v2

  • Code length shortcut (22.7% vs. 12.5% chance): Symmetric ±10-char decoy filtering centered gold length in Code (prose domains sit at chance). Removing all 162 Code items preserves r = +0.9979, ρ = +0.9956 across all 105 live models, leaves the top-9 AUC set invariant, and leaves the top-9 MRR ordering 100% identical. Fixed in v2 by removing length filtering.
  • Mandatory-reasoning zero-think tax (BYPASS): Six open endpoints (Qwen3.8 2.4T-A95B, Muse Spark Open, Inkling 2 Small, Reflection Beam 1.0, gpt-oss-120b/20b) reject 0-CoT on standard chat APIs and required raw /completions zero-think templates—imposing a verified −0.071 AUC cold-start tax (z = 2.07). Kept visible on the leaderboard (badged BYPASS) and excluded from the 14-anchor native-chat OLS fit by default (17-anchor sensitivity LOLO = 3.65% across 9 labs).
  • Cross-lab rank order vs. AA-Omniscience-Acc: While ANPI beats AA-Omniscience-Acc on predictive error across both OpenAI (0.85% vs. 3.27%) and Elie (3.41% 14-model / 3.70% 13-shared vs. 4.72% LOLO), AA-Omniscience-Acc slightly edges cross-lab Spearman ρ (+0.901 vs. +0.842).
  • ANPI v2 plan: Scale to N = 2,500 (1 item per URL, cutting the 71% finite-N floor to ~0.55%), replace single-model decoys with a 3–4 model base-checkpoint decoy ensemble, add fresh Fiction + multilingual slices (ZH/DE/FR/JA), and run rolling monthly snapshots.
