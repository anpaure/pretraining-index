# Independent review of the Anpaure Pretraining Index v1

The native score is reproducible as an eight-choice continuation-ranking benchmark. The evidence supports an association with published pretraining losses. It does not establish how accurately the index predicts the base loss of a new frontier model.

## What reproduced

I rebuilt the HTML and JSON in a temporary directory. Both matched the published artifacts byte for byte. I independently parsed the saved answer letters, joined each response to its gold candidate, and recovered all 29 evaluated endpoints’ displayed MRR and top-one accuracy from 27,256 valid model–item rankings. All 954 gold snippets and prefixes also reproduced from their archived source documents. The 14 existing tests passed. I made no paid inference calls.

All seven OpenAI anchor values match the publisher’s chart. Both imported scales for all 27 Bakouch models match a separate calculation from the publisher’s underlying data to floating-point precision. The Gemma 26B estimate using only the other 13 anchors also reproduces at 0.58722 versus 0.59036 measured BPB. OpenAI measures next-token loss on a fixed internal code snapshot. [OpenAI measurement](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/) · [Bakouch index](https://eliebak.com/pretraining-index)

## Findings that affect interpretation

1. **Ranks do not identify pretraining loss.** The index measures how a deployed chat model orders one authentic snippet and seven generated alternatives. Identical rankings can result from very different probabilities on the authentic text and hence very different losses. Even monotone sharpening of the model probabilities can reverse the ordering of their ratios to nonuniform decoy probabilities. For two candidates, probabilities of (0.6, 0.4) and decoy probabilities of (0.65, 0.35) favor the second candidate by likelihood ratio. Squaring and renormalizing the model probabilities favors the first. Decoy length filters also explicitly depend on the gold snippet. The seven-OpenAI-anchor correlation of 0.9916 is real, but it cannot establish the asserted invariance or the accuracy of closed-model cross-lab predictions. [Dashboard methodology](../build_anpaure_index_v1.py:640) · [Decoy construction](../generate_decoys_v1.py:235)
2. **Code candidate lengths give away information.** On the 162 code items, picking the candidate closest to the median character length of the eight choices scores 22.7% top-one accuracy, counting ties fractionally. Random choice gives 12.5%. This rule reads neither the prefix nor the candidate text. A bootstrap over the 116 distinct commit URLs gives a descriptive 95% interval of 20.1%–25.2%. The check is exploratory. It demonstrates a shortcut on this benchmark and does not establish which models used it. [Dashboard claim](../build_anpaure_index_v1.py:679)
3. **Four leading endpoints exceed the OpenAI calibration range.** GPT-6.2 Astra is the strongest OpenAI anchor at 0.7125 MRR. Gemini Carbon (0.7370), Claude Fable (0.7356), Gemini Neon (0.7339) and Gemini Argon (0.7208) all lie beyond it. Ten of the 29 measured endpoints are outside the range at either end. Leaving one open model out gives 2.69% mean absolute percentage error on Bakouch’s October scale. Leaving out each entire lab in turn gives 3.20%, with a worst model error of 8.29%. Both diagnostics concern the available open anchors. The seven-model OpenAI error of 1.02% tests only within OpenAI. [Calibration and dashboard claim](../build_anpaure_index_v1.py:535)
4. **Coverage affects the apparent winner.** Individual scores use 723–954 valid items. The 11 featured pairs share 927–953 items each. All 29 endpoints share 599. On those 599, Fable scores 0.7275, Neon 0.7217 and Carbon 0.7094. Carbon leads on the main display. On the 950 items shared by Carbon and Fable alone, Fable minus Carbon is −0.0025 MRR with a paired 95% interval of [−0.0228, +0.0179]. For uncertainty estimates, 92 code snippets also share commit URLs with another item. Resampling by source would account for that dependence. [Pairwise calculation](../build_anpaure_index_v1.py:118)
5. **The dashboard and runner have different validity rules.** The dashboard was updated during this review. I verified that its current permutation check excludes the two malformed DeepSeek rankings found in the earlier build. The standalone runner still fills in missing letters and includes incomplete responses in its model summaries. For example, the saved Inkling summary counts 728 responses, of which 723 qualify for the dashboard. The “Zero-CoT” filter also retains models with a mean below ten reasoning tokens, including GPT-6.2 Astra with positive reported reasoning on 22 of 954 items. Absent telemetry defaults to zero. [Ranking parser](../pplx_protocol_a/nce_bpb.py:71) · [Runner aggregation](../run_anpaure_v1.py:283) · [Dashboard filter](../build_anpaure_index_v1.py:584)
