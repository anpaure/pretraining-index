External calibration

Solid circles are published anchor ground truths used to fit the 2-parameter log-BPB Thurstone ruler ln(BPB) = a + b·(−d′); diamonds are out-of-sample projections.

Why Pairwise AUC + Thurstone d′ onto log-BPB. Across all 99,921 real rank rows, candidate scores follow Thurstone Case V (Gaussian sums of token log-probs, 6.8× lower KL than Plackett-Luce), and tail ranks r ≥ 2 retain partial correlation +0.73–+0.76 with true BPB after controlling for Top-1. Combining Thurstone d′ = √2 Φ−1(AUC) with Ruan et al. (NeurIPS 2024) yields the 2-parameter law ln(BPB) = a − b·d′—achieving r = +0.990, ρ = +0.988, LOO = 0.85% across all 10 published OpenAI models and r = +0.911, LOO = 2.87%, LOLO = 3.41% across Elie’s 14 open models (7 labs), with zero statistically significant ceiling curvature across 4 independent datasets.

Extrapolation above anchor ranges. On OpenAI’s ruler, Claude Fable 5.2 (0.8550) and Gemini 4 Carbon (0.8482) exceed top anchor GPT-6.2 Astra (0.8444); on Elie’s open ruler, the top closed tier exceeds Kimi K3.1 Pro (0.7518). Every model outside its anchor span is flagged EXTRAP with exact exponentiated Student-t intervals.

100% Zero-CoT enforcement & template-bypass rule. All 99 native-chat endpoints answered at 0 reasoning tokens on standard /api/v1/chat/completions. Mandatory-reasoning endpoints that reject zero-CoT on chat APIs (Muse Spark Open, Inkling 2 Small, Reflection Beam 1.0, gpt-oss-120b/20b) were evaluated via raw /api/v1/completions zero-think templates and remain visible here (badged BYPASS), but are excluded from the 14-anchor OLS ruler by default (cold-start bypass tax; blind holdouts +3.5% / +5.9% / +6.4%; 17-anchor sensitivity LOLO = 3.65%).

How is this calculated?

Black-box 8-choice continuation ranking across 5 Nov-2026 domains—no logprobs, no echo prompts, strictly audited 8-letter permutations.

  1. Post-cutoff multi-domain corpus (N=954, 908 unique source URLs): All documents carry Nov-2026 timestamps across 5 domains—199 News/Speeches (UK Hansard & BBC), 198 Papers (arXiv & bioRxiv), 198 Essays (Guardian & LessWrong), 197 newly created Wikipedia articles, and 162 newly added systems code blocks (116 distinct commits across CPython, Linux, Postgres, Go, LLVM, PyTorch, etc.).
  2. Neutral 24B cloud decoys (K=8): At a cut point inside each document, we keep the authentic next snippet (~6 prose words or ~36 chars of single-line code) and sample 7 distinct T = 1.0 raw continuations from mistral-small-24b-instruct-2501 (zero family overlap with any ranked model, trailing-space BPE bug fixed). Decoys in v1 were length-filtered around the gold length (see Code length caveat).
  3. Zero-logprob 8-way ranking & RL sharpening note: Each API receives the shuffled 8 choices A..H and must return a strict 8-distinct-letter permutation (C,A,F,B,H,D,E,G). Global monotone temperature sharpening of a model’s distribution preserves the ordering of candidate likelihoods p(y|x) (unlike raw top-token cross-entropy)—though note it does not preserve likelihood-ratio ordering p(y|x)/q(y|x) when q is nonuniform, and rankings alone do not uniquely identify absolute generative loss without anchor calibration.
  4. Native AUC & Thurstone log-BPB rulers: Native score is pairwise rank AUC = (8 − mean_rank) / 7 (exact probability of preferring the real continuation over a random decoy; chance = 0.500, zero fitted parameters) with source-URL cluster bootstrap CIs; MRR and Top-1 remain reported alongside it. External OpenAI and Elie views fit a 2-parameter log-BPB OLS ruler ln(BPB) = a + b·(−d′) where d′ = √2 Φ−1(AUC), with exact exponentiated Student-t outer intervals and strict EXTRAP badges outside the anchor range.
Trace hygiene & telemetry: Both the standalone runner and this dashboard require an exact 8-distinct-letter permutation. Models that wrote truncated prose preambles on >10 items were retried identically with a strict no-prose rule at r_tok = 0 (verified not to inflate scores: Fable 5.2 moved 0.7367 → 0.7356 MRR). OpenRouter omits reasoning-token fields on endpoints that do not report them (defaulting to 0); exact positive-r_tok item counts are shown in the ledger.
